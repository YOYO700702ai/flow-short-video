## 鏡 1（6 秒）

```
Starting from the attached image. In a single continuous shot, no scene cuts. The camera stays fixed; the cottage and the three little pigs stay the same size and in the same place in every frame. A warm hand-painted storybook cartoon, the same style as the image. [0-2s] The mother pig steps out of the cottage door toward the three little pigs, wiping her hooves on her white apron. The little pig in the blue cap bounces on the spot twice, the fat little pig in the straw hat gives a huge sleepy yawn, the smallest pig stands still and straight. [2-6s] The mother pig stops in front of them and smiles; morning birds flutter past. A gentle female storyteller narrates off-screen in Mandarin Chinese, warm and slow: 從前，豬媽媽有三隻小豬。 None of the pigs speak; all their mouths stay closed. Sound design: morning birdsong, soft breeze. No music. No subtitles. No on-screen text.
```
